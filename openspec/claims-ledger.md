# Claims ledger

Source: the article "Build the Case, Let the Clinician Decide", in [docs/article-draft.md](../docs/article-draft.md) (latest export, 2026-10-16, with four checks and the "decision is code" line). Every row below was re-checked against that export on 2026-10-16.

Each row is one checkable claim the article makes about this design, and the evidence that proves it. The work is done when every row has evidence.

- **Evidence types:**
  - **test:** an automated test in this repository.
  - **deployed:** a resource that exists in the AWS account, shown by a recorded listing.
  - **recorded:** a captured call and its response.
  - **report:** a generated report committed with its raw data.
  - **citation:** an external primary source.
- **Status values:** `open`, `proven`, or `flag: article edit`.
- **The rule:** if a row can't be proven, the article changes. The evidence never stretches to fit.
- **Change:** the OpenSpec change in `openspec/changes/` that delivers the evidence.

## Scope: the five-minute demo (PM, 2026-10-16)

The remaining work delivers six demo beats and nothing more. Deferred items are listed in the README's "Known limitations" and marked below as `deferred: known limitation`.

1. **Both gateways, side by side** (`gateway-and-policy`): the agent gateway's five case-building tools and no denial tool; the reviewer gateway's `record_adverse_determination` and `record_clinician_approval`, for clinicians only. Rows B1, B2, A2.
2. **Clean case** (`case-packet-and-failure-routing`): the Strands agent (three gathering tools, Nova Pro) builds a cited packet, and `decide()` approves. Rows G1–G7, G9–G11, B6, B7, A1.
3. **Counterfactual** (`case-packet-and-failure-routing`): one note removed; the case is routed to the reviewer with the gap marked. Rows G3, G7, F5–F7.
4. **Agent refused** (`gateway-and-policy`) at the reviewer gateway's authorizer and by Cedar. Rows B3, B4, B5, A3. Proven.
5. **Clinician succeeds** (`clinician-workbench`) with the same tool, from the local workbench page. The decision record chains to the recommendation record. Demoed right after beat 4. Rows B8, L3, L6, L7.
6. **Clean trace** (`evidence-record-and-trace`): a canary scan with a positive control. Rows L1, L2, A6.

**After Monday** (`evaluation-harness`): the harness report, with six cases × two models × three trials. Rows M1–M8.

**Deferred** (`post-monday-hardening`): every row marked `deferred: known limitation`.

Also kept: one evidence bucket with the hash chain and its verify script (L4, L6, L8), and one verified teardown on Sunday.

**Schedule (PM, 2026-10-16).**
- **Monday's demo:** beats 1–5 plus the clean-trace scan.
- **Before Monday:** beats 1–5, the canary scan with its positive control, the shared evidence work, and one verified teardown on Sunday.
- **After Monday:** the harness run and report, and the Sonnet 4.6 comparison (the M rows).
- **Checkpoint, Saturday 18:00:** if beat 2 does not work end to end, beat 5 becomes a scripted clinician call through the reviewer gateway, plus a screenshot.
- **Publication:** the article is published only after the ledger is green.
