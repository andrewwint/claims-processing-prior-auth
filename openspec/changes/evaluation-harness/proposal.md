# Proposal

## Why

The agent cannot deny care, so it has only two possible mistakes:
- **It approves a case a clinician would not have approved.** That is the safety error.
- **It routes a case it could have approved.** That is a cost.

The article commits to measuring the first, the false auto-approval rate, on counterfactual synthetic cases, and to publishing the number whatever it is. It is priority 5.

The design moved the decision into code. `decide(packet)` and the approval service approve only what passes the four checks against trusted sources. So the harness measures the whole system, model plus checks, not the model's judgment alone. What it can show is this:
- whether any case labeled "needs review" still gets through (false auto-approval)
- how many clearly covered cases still get approved under strict matching (auto-approval)

## What Changes

- **Pre-registration** (`harness/PREREGISTRATION.md`), committed before the first run. It sets out:
  - the questions, the case set and its provenance, and the models
  - trials per case and per model
  - the metric definitions, scoring rules and stopping rules
  - the exact wording the report may use
- **Case set**, generated here from the synthetic data (Larkspur Health Plan and Tidewell Mutual; about 8 patients across two tenants). Each case records its provenance: its parent case, and the one change made.
  - **Clean approvals:** parents labeled "approve".
  - **Single-element counterfactuals:** one piece of required evidence removed, or changed to fall below a minimum, with everything else fixed. Labeled "needs review".
  - **Forged documents:**
    - a document that is not in the chart index
    - an indexed document whose text was altered after indexing
    - a document whose kind doesn't match the criterion

    Labeled "needs review".
  - **Wrong patient:** another patient's document placed in this case's chart, or cited by it. Labeled "needs review".
  - **Wrong plan:** the request names a payer or CPT with no compiled policy, or the member is not eligible. Labeled "needs review".
- **Models.**
  - Amazon Nova Pro (us-east-1), the runtime default.
  - Claude Sonnet 4.6 through the US inference profile, the documented opt-in, exercised here.
  - Each gets a real access call before the run; both are already recorded.
- **Runner.** Runs the real pipeline code in-process: extraction by the model, `decide()` in code, tool calls to the **deployed** gateways with Policy enforced, and approvals through the approval service. Each trial records:
  - the sampling settings as sent
  - the stop reason
  - the decision, and the approval service's verdict
  - any policy denial
- **Report.**
  - The false auto-approval rate and the auto-approval rate per model, with numerator, denominator and N next to every number.
  - Every trial, with truncation counted as a failure.
  - The cases labeled synthetic and author-labeled.
  - It is regenerated byte-identically from the raw trials, committed unedited, whatever the rate.

## Impact

- **New code:** `harness/` (case generator, runner, scorer, report) and tests.
- **AWS usage:** model calls and gateway calls only, under the budget. The deployed gateways and approval service must be up during the run.
- **Depends on** `case-packet-and-failure-routing` and `gateway-and-policy`.
